I am a PhD candidate at [BUAA](https://www.buaa.edu.cn/) and [Shanghai AI Laboratory](https://www.shlab.org.cn/), advised by Prof. [Yu Qiao](https://mmlab.siat.ac.cn/yuqiao/) and Prof. [Xiao Sun](https://jimmysuen.github.io/). My research focuses on spatial intelligence&mdash;from efficient 3D Gaussian Splatting to agentic 3D understanding&mdash;and autoregressive 3D generative models.

Previously I was a research assistant at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), where I developed efficient token representations for Vision Transformers. I earned my bachelor's degree from the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/).
